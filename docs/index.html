Step-by-Step Installation & Setup

Three simple steps to unleash system-wide writing correction on your Android device.

1

Install WordPilot APK

Download the signed release APK WordPilot-v1.0.apk and tap to install on your Android device (Android 8.0 through Android 15/16).

Download Official Release APK (v1.0)
2

Turn On Accessibility Service

Open WordPilot ➔ tap "Floating Assistant (Cross-App)" ➔ select "Open Accessibility Settings" ➔ tap WordPilot Assistant under Downloaded Apps and toggle to ON.

💡 HyperOS / ColorOS Note: If Android displays "Restricted setting", go to Phone Settings ➔ Apps ➔ WordPilot ➔ tap 3 dots (upper right) ➔ tap "Allow restricted settings", then re-toggle.
3

Configure Your Grammar Provider

In API Providers & Keys, paste your free Sapling AI key for contextual grammar, or use LanguageTool out-of-the-box with zero setup required.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Is WordPilot completely free?

Yes! WordPilot is 100% free and open-source under the Apache 2.0 license. The LanguageTool fallback requires zero payment or keys, and Sapling provides a generous free monthly quota.

Does WordPilot store or track my keystrokes?

Never. WordPilot contains zero telemetry, analytics, or background tracking. Corrections are processed solely through your configured APIs, and over 50+ banking apps and password fields are strictly shielded.

How does the 30% edge-tuck feature work?

When minimized, the bubble automatically snaps to the left or right screen border and rests 30% tucked inside the bezel. When you tap it, it smoothly expands into visible screen bounds with safe margins.

Can I host my own LanguageTool server offline?

Yes! Simply run docker run -d -p 8010:8010 erikvl87/languagetool on your PC or home server and input your server's local IP address into WordPilot for 100% private, offline usage.
